# Eight broad tissue labels

The retained Benchmark 1 training set has 397 H&E images. Before opening the 87 development validation masks for this reanalysis, the training expert masks were used to select eight broad classes: tumor, stroma, necrosis, epithelium, blood, vessel, adipose, and immune infiltrate. Each reported class has at least 20 independent training source groups and 500,000 training mask pixels. Alveolar tissue, cartilage or bone, liver tissue, mucus/plasma/fluids, and muscle remain unscored because no defensible, sufficiently represented broad parent was available. They are tissue, not background. The eight labels cover 96.332% of the original annotated training pixels and 97.212% of the previously scorable expert pixels in the 87 validation images.

The supervised Virchow2 model selected 256 of 1,280 channels and a regularized multinomial logistic head (C=0.1) using source-group-disjoint training splits. The fitted head, dataset eligibility rule, 1.5 µm ROI contour smoothing, and 400 µm² small-region repair were then applied to all 87 development images. Mean expert-present case macro IoU was 0.694; the equal-weight mean across the four multiclass datasets was 0.603. The prior 24-class supervised head mapped to the same eight classes scored 0.692 overall and 0.604 across the four multiclass datasets. These differences are small, and the broader score is a different task from the earlier 24-class score. The validation images had been inspected during prior development, so these numbers are not an untouched test result.

The earlier KODAMA–Bora + fastPLS full pixel confusion matrices were exactly remapped and rescored: mean expert-present case macro IoU 0.267 over all 87, and 0.301 across the four multiclass datasets. Its group geometry and classifier were not refitted. Its exact spatial group masks are absent from the curated local cache, so no replacement broad-label overlay was inferred from compressed web images. The four external methods' existing outputs were also mapped and rescored without retraining. MedSAM remains a prompted three-class method; scores across methods are not a fair common-task ranking.

The 13 public Benchmark 2 whole slides show the new eight-class supervised head at full overview and nine matched zooms. Existing KODAMA–Bora geometry and external predictions were mapped to broad classes. All six overlays use the same final Bora tissue support for display. The whole slides have no independent whole-slide expert masks and therefore no accuracy scores.

The [case-level development reanalysis](benchmark1-development-uncertainty-2026-11-12.md) reports uncertainty intervals, paired change against the preceding head, scored-pixel coverage and per-class support. These analyses do not convert the inspected development images into an independent test.
