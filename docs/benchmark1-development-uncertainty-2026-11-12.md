# Benchmark 1 development reanalysis

The eight-class supervised Virchow2 model was fitted on 397 retained expert-masked training images. The 87 evaluation images were inspected during method development, so the following values describe that development set; they are not an independent generalization test. Source-group split IDs are not yet verified patient IDs.

The image-equal mean of each case's expert-present macro IoU was **0.694** (10,000-resample case-bootstrap interval **0.646–0.742**). Only **73.4%** of all image pixels were in the scored ontology and expert region. The equally weighted mean of the four multiclass datasets, excluding the 19 single-class PEsO images, was **0.603** (source-stratified case-bootstrap interval **0.570–0.634**). All 19 PEsO images scored 1.0 under a training-defined source-dataset class gate; this does not demonstrate detection of unanticipated tissue classes.

The preceding 24-role head, remapped to eight labels, scored 0.692. The paired mean difference for the current head was **+0.002** (case-bootstrap interval **−0.015–0.020**): 28 cases improved, 19 tied and 40 worsened. There is no demonstrated improvement on these inspected images. Blood and vessel remain weak: mean case IoU 0.175 on 17 expert-present cases and 0.141 on 10, respectively. The [machine-readable aggregate](benchmark1-development-reanalysis-2026-11-12.json) includes class denominators, dataset means, source hashes and all comparator means.

The KODAMA–Bora plus fastPLS and external-method scores in that aggregate were obtained by mapping frozen outputs into the broad ontology. Different supervision, class coverage and MedSAM prompts prevent treating those rows as a matched method ranking. The whole-slide views have no independent expert reference masks. The separate 20-context PUMA melanoma evaluation had five complete predictions; its 0.514 conditional mean IoU is accompanied by a zero-assigned 20-context sensitivity mean of 0.129. Fifteen technical noncompletions remain.

The [pure-Python case-receipt audit](../methods/reduced-labels/audit_development_metrics.py) accepts six frozen JSON receipts: `--supervised`, `--prior-24-head`, `--kodama-bora`, `--external`, `--ontology`, `--original-supervised`, plus `--out`. Its output was byte-identical to the aggregate linked above when run against the versioned local receipts. Raw images, masks and case-level receipts are not redistributed here; their absence limits public independent reproduction. The audit does not retrain a method or constitute an untouched test.
