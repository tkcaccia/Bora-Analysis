# Benchmark 2 full-slide comparator publication

The [public method implementation and parameter record](../methods/README.md)
links the training and inference scripts used for the six displayed methods,
their checkpoint identities, and the external source repositories.

As of 11 November 2026, [the public gallery](https://tkcaccia.github.io/Bora-Analysis/visium-hd/comparators/) contains 11 verified public-source H&E whole-slide comparisons. Every published page shows the same calibrated H&E frame alongside class-aware U-Net, DeepLabV3+, MedSAM and VISTA-PATH outputs. The web images are downsampled previews of complete full-resolution prediction masks. All four overlays on each page use the same Bora-derived tissue/background support for display. Eight slides have final Bora tissue-group geometry; three TCGA slides without final geometry use Bora's 4 µm H&E-derived support rule.

U-Net and DeepLabV3+ use checkpoints fitted on the 397 retained Benchmark 1 training images and expert masks. On whole slides, MedSAM receives tumor, stroma and necrosis boxes derived from the class-aware U-Net prediction for the same tile; it is a prompted three-role output. The Benchmark 1 development comparison used an earlier U-Net for MedSAM boxes. VISTA-PATH uses an externally pretrained, prompt-free 13-class vocabulary, with matching roles mapped to shared display colors. The method legends list classes visible in each preview.

These slides have no independent whole-slide expert masks. The gallery is for visual inspection and does not establish Dice, IoU, accuracy, or a ranking of the four methods. Patch boundaries within tissue remain visible where produced. The shared support changes only the displayed previews; the full-resolution predictions are unchanged.

The [13 integrated public whole-slide pages](https://tkcaccia.github.io/Bora-Analysis/visium-hd/) show H&E, KODAMA–Bora, supervised Virchow2 Bora, U-Net, DeepLabV3+, MedSAM and VISTA-PATH at full overview and nine matched zooms. The current supervised head uses eight broad classes selected from the frozen 397 Benchmark 1 training images and expert targets. Five sparsely represented tissue categories are unscored. KODAMA–Bora geometry and the four external-model predictions were mapped into the broad labels without retraining. The union of each slide's final Bora tissue-group polygons clips all six displayed overlays to the same tissue support. These views are exploratory and carry no whole-slide expert accuracy or method ranking. The separate four-method gallery remains available for full-slide comparator inspection.

All 12 slides in the frozen local comparator campaign passed the independent streaming source and mask audit: 48 full-resolution prediction TIFFs. The 11 public-source previews are published; the user-supplied PM016 slide remains local and is not included on this site or in the manuscript. The site publication has per-slide page and asset SHA256 receipts in the local Bora project. These results remain exploratory visual comparisons without whole-slide expert accuracy.
