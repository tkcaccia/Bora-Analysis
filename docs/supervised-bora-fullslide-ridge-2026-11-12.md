# Supervised Bora ridge whole-slide masks

The 13 public Benchmark 2 whole-slide pages show the training-selected eight-class Virchow2 ridge head on calibrated H&E images. The model was fitted on 397 retained Benchmark 1 training images and uses 203 selected channels from each verified 1,280-channel feature cache. It is separate from the anonymous KODAMA–Bora groups and the four external model predictions, which remain pinned.

A 2% whole-slide class-presence floor was selected using source-group-disjoint held-out training cases only. Across 149 such cases, mean false-positive classes fell from 1.215 to 0.362, while mean expert-present case macro IoU changed from 0.683 to 0.680. The rule suppresses classes winning fewer than 2% of tissue score-grid tokens; those pixels take the next-highest retained supervised score. No development or whole-slide expert annotation was used to select the floor.

The executed [whole-slide sources](../methods/benchmark2/supervised_ridge/) verify model, feature, H&E registration and reviewed Bora tissue geometry identities before projecting scores across the complete calibrated slide. They perform a preparatory area repair, 6 µm class-contour smoothing, final 2,000 µm² repair and native-coordinate vector conversion. Each slide has one full-resolution TIFF and GeoJSON. All ten web views are crops of that final mask and use the same Bora support as the other displayed methods for background presentation.

An independent local audit streamed every TIFF pixel against its exact raster across all 13 slides: no differences or positive pixels outside reviewed support. The per-slide vector audit found exact native GeoJSON raster round trips and no retained component below 2,000 µm². All 130 web assets were hash checked; overview and one detailed field per slide were visually inspected for registration. Live page and asset SHA256 receipts are recorded separately. These checks verify output identity and alignment, not tissue classification accuracy: no independent whole-slide expert masks are available.
