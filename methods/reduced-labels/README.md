# Eight broad tissue labels

These are the exact Chiamaka analysis scripts used for the 11 November 2026 label-space reanalysis. `TRAINING_SUPPORT.json` records aggregate counts from 397 retained training expert masks. `ONTOLOGY_FROZEN.json` fixes the eight broad labels before development validation predictions were opened. The scoring rule omits rare original roles from the denominator; those pixels remain tissue, never background.

`train_supervised.py` reads the frozen full-channel training token sample, selects 256 of 1,280 Virchow2 channels on two source-group-disjoint training splits, and fits the eight-class logistic head. `validate_supervised.py` applies that head to 87 development images. `rescore_existing_comparators.py` merges existing external-model predictions into the broad roles. `rescore_bora_confusion.py` exactly remaps the saved original KODAMA–Bora plus fastPLS pixel confusion matrices, including missed tissue. `audit_training_support.py` and `freeze_ontology.py` produced the published aggregate support and ontology receipts.

The scripts retain their original local path bindings to make the executed source identifiable. To rerun them elsewhere, supply the licensed source images, expert masks, full-channel feature caches, calibration receipts, and frozen sample/split manifests at equivalent paths, then update the root constants in an isolated copy. Those inputs and trained weights are not redistributed. The four external checkpoints and KODAMA–Bora/fastPLS model were **not retrained** for the eight classes; their broad-label results are a remapping and rescore. The independent whole-slide projection and rendering sources are in the versioned local Bora migration record. The 13 public whole slides have no expert whole-slide accuracy reference.

`audit_development_metrics.py` is a portable receipt-only audit that reports development case-bootstrap intervals, scored-pixel coverage, per-class support and a paired comparison with the preceding head. It takes six frozen case receipts as named arguments and produced the [published aggregate](../../docs/benchmark1-development-reanalysis-2026-11-12.json) byte-for-byte from the local inputs. The case-level source receipts themselves are retained locally and are not redistributed.
