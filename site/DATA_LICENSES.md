# Viewer data attribution and reuse

No blanket code license is applied to dataset images, annotations or their derivatives.
The original data providers retain their rights. See attribution.html for creator
credits, original publications, source links and modifications.

* BCSS: Amgad, Elfandy and colleagues, Bioinformatics 2019,
  https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btz083 ; TCGA-BRCA imagery.
  https://github.com/PathologyDataScience/BCSS . MIT in that repository concerns
  software; it does not replace the original TCGA image terms.
* TIGER: van Rijthoven and colleagues,
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6014422 . All annotations and RUMC/Jules Bordet
  images: CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ .
  TCGA images retain their original terms. Original notice:
  https://zenodo.org/records/6014422/files/license.txt?download=1 .
* GCSS: Jiangbo Shi, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8176354 . Zenodo release:
  CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Acknowledge
  TCGA-STAD as the underlying image source and the GCSS annotation provider.
* IGNITE: Spronck, van Eekelen, van Midden and colleagues,
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17735903 . CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . IGNITE-derived previews,
  overlays, converted RGB images and masks here are adaptations under these
  same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Changes include resizing, color mapping,
  anonymous tiling, prediction and polygon simplification.

There is no implied endorsement. Previews are for research inspection.
Downloaded GeoJSONs use native ROI pixel coordinates; images are resized for
browser display. Dataset assignment for every asset is recorded in data.json.
BUILD_PROVENANCE.json records exact copied-asset SHA256 values.
