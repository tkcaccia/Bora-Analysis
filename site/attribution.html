The viewer displays resized H&E ROI previews, expert annotation overlays, reference-derived anonymous tile masks, transformed RGB previews and predicted tissue masks. These are research visualizations derived from the datasets below. Original providers retain their rights; the repository does not apply a blanket software license to dataset images or annotations.

BCSS · 42 evaluation ROIs

Mohamed Amgad, Habiba Elfandy and colleagues. Structured crowdsourcing enables convolutional segmentation of histology images. Bioinformatics (2019), doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btz083. Images originate from TCGA-BRCA. See the official BCSS repository and TCGA Research Network. The BCSS repository’s MIT software license must not be interpreted as a license for the underlying TCGA images. Attribute both the annotation providers and TCGA when reusing these derivatives.

TIGER · 26 evaluation ROIs

Mart van Rijthoven, Witali Aswolinskiy, Leslie Tessier, Maschenka Balkenhol, Joep Bogaerts, Jeroen van der Laak, Roberto Salgado and Francesco Ciompi, with the contributing pathologists. TIGER training dataset, ROI-level annotations. The provider’s license notice applies CC BY-NC 4.0 to all annotations and to RUMC/Jules Bordet images. TCGA images retain their original TCGA terms. TCGA-derived TIGER annotations include BCSS and NuCLS contributions. See CC BY-NC 4.0.

GCSS · 24 evaluation ROIs

Jiangbo Shi. Gastric Cancer Semantic Segmentation dataset, released with the semi-supervised pixel contrastive learning study. The Zenodo release is CC BY 4.0. Images originate from TCGA-STAD; acknowledge TCGA and the annotation provider. See SSPCL.

IGNITE · 24 evaluation ROIs

Joey Matheus Antonius Spronck, Leander van Eekelen, Dominique van Midden and colleagues, led by Francesco Ciompi. IGNITE data toolkit, version 2. Data are released under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The IGNITE-derived previews, overlays and predicted masks in this viewer are adaptations and are provided under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Changes include display resizing, annotation-color mapping, reference-derived tiling, RGB conversion, segmentation prediction and polygon simplification. No endorsement by the providers is implied.

Visium HD colon whole-slide example

The full-slide H&E image is from 10x Genomics, Visium HD Spatial Gene Expression Library, Human Colorectal Cancer (FFPE), which lists the dataset under CC BY 4.0. The separate expert GeoJSON was supplied for this study. The displayed WSI previews are resized and overlaid with Bora predictions; the full-resolution source and annotation are linked from the study documentation.

Reading the results

The 116 images retain their original split labels (78 test and 38 validation), and constitute an exploratory benchmark rather than an independent final validation. BCSS and TIGER have overlapping source material and should not be interpreted as independent disease cohorts. Source-class names and excluded annotation codes are shown with each image.

Only tissue-region annotations are used. PUMA is excluded from both evaluation splits. Unannotated tissue is processed but excluded from quality scores. Previews are resized; the displayed per-axis resolution and GeoJSON coordinates refer to the native ROI.
