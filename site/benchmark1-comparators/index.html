Benchmark 1 · broad tissue labels

Browse 87 H&E development validation images with eight tissue labels selected from the 397 training images. The new supervised Bora mask and its black-and-white Virchow2 responses appear with the expert and external methods on each image.

Training data · public expert masksValidation data · 87 images

Development evaluation. These 87 images were inspected during earlier method development and are not an untouched test. The eight-class label set and supervised model were selected on training images alone. Alveolar tissue, cartilage or bone, liver tissue, mucus/plasma/fluids, and muscle are unscored because they lacked enough independent training support; they are not treated as background. Broader labels change the scoring task, so compare scores only within this page.

The 19 PEsO images have one scorable expert class. The four other datasets give a more informative multiclass summary. Scores are mean IoU over expert-present broad classes; unscored expert pixels are excluded.

Broad-label results on the same H&E image

The legend names only classes in the expert mask or at least 1% of a displayed model panel. Model overlays use the same Bora H&E tissue support. The KODAMA–Bora spatial mask is unavailable in the curated local cache, so that panel is deliberately unfilled; its score comes from exact frozen expert-by-prediction pixel counts.

The supervised Bora head was refitted on training-only eight-class Virchow2 data. KODAMA–Bora geometry and fastPLS naming were not refitted; their full confusion matrices were remapped to these broad labels. Their scores are therefore a reanalysis of the frozen output. The external predictions were similarly remapped and rescored without retraining, and MedSAM predicts only three prompted classes.

External method scores

U-Net and DeepLabV3+ use RGB H&E. VISTA-PATH uses external pretraining. MedSAM uses U-Net-derived boxes for tumor, stroma and necrosis. These methods have different output tasks; the cards do not establish a method ranking.
