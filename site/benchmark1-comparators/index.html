Benchmark 1 · broad tissue labels

Browse 87 H&E development validation images with eight broad tissue labels selected from the 397 training images. Supervised Bora masks and class response maps appear with the expert and external methods on each image. PEsO uses a separate two-class head selected on its training slides.

Training data · public expert masksValidation data · 87 images

Development evaluation. These 87 images were inspected during earlier method development and are not an untouched test. The eight-class label set and supervised model were selected on training images alone. Alveolar tissue, cartilage or bone, liver tissue, mucus/plasma/fluids, and muscle are unscored because they lacked enough independent training support; they are not treated as background. Broader labels change the scoring task, so compare scores only within this page.

PEsO corrected reference and supervised result. The 19 PEsO figures show original expert epithelium and a conservative, H&E-supported weak stroma proxy for some non-epithelial pixels. This proxy is not a pathologist annotation. A 64-channel supervised Bora head was selected using only 18 source-disjoint PEsO training slides and scored on these 19 inspected development images. The external predictions were rescored on the same two-class reference without retraining. The former epithelium-only 100% score is withdrawn. KODAMA–Bora cannot be rescored on PEsO because its exact spatial mask is absent.

Matched Benchmark 1 quality scores

The primary analysis scores native masks from Bora, a newly fitted eight-output U-Net, and a newly fitted eight-output DeepLabV3+ on the same 68 expert-reference ROIs. All 68 completed. The 19 PEsO ROIs are shown separately: epithelium is expert annotated, whereas stroma is an H&E-derived weak proxy. These are previously inspected development images, not an independent test. Union-present IoU scores tissue names and penalizes a class predicted only by the method. Boundary F1 and symmetric region covering ignore tissue names: they measure whether the internal tissue partitions align even when labels differ. HD95 measures boundary distance (lower is better). These geometry metrics do not establish cell-level segmentation. All scores use positive annotated tissue pixels; reference zero mixes glass, excluded and unannotated tissue, so a reference-based background score is unavailable. The common H&E-support-clipped sensitivity analysis is available in the downloadable data.

The PEsO-specific ridge head was selected by full-mask performance on three disjoint training folds, then fitted on all 18 PEsO training slides. Its PEsO development score uses expert epithelium and weak proxy stroma; the four other datasets retain the earlier global head and original expert references. Historical displayed-panel IoUs for this image

For BCSS, GCSS, IGNITE and TIGER, supervised Bora selects 32 discriminative channels per class from the full 1,280-channel Virchow2 cache (203 unique channels), then fits a multiclass ridge classifier on 397 training images. For PEsO, a separate 64-channel binary ridge head uses the same full-channel cache and training-only source-disjoint model selection. No development image was used for feature, model or threshold selection. KODAMA–Bora geometry and fastPLS naming were not refitted; their original-reference scores remain a reanalysis of frozen counts for the other four datasets. External predictions were rescored on the PEsO reference without retraining.
