Benchmark 1 · broad tissue labels

Browse 87 H&E development validation images with eight tissue labels selected from the 397 training images. The training-selected supervised Bora mask and its eight black-and-white Virchow2 responses appear with the expert and external methods on each image.

Training data · public expert masksValidation data · 87 images

Development evaluation. These 87 images were inspected during earlier method development and are not an untouched test. The eight-class label set and supervised model were selected on training images alone. Alveolar tissue, cartilage or bone, liver tissue, mucus/plasma/fluids, and muscle are unscored because they lacked enough independent training support; they are not treated as background. Broader labels change the scoring task, so compare scores only within this page.

PEsO annotation limitation. The 19 PEsO reference masks label epithelium but do not provide expert stroma labels. The PEsO figures and scores below still use those original masks and the earlier supervised Bora model. A conservative, H&E-supported weak-stroma reference has been prepared for a new training and development rerun; it is not an expert stroma annotation. Corrected results will replace these panels only after the rerun and its audit are complete.

Supervised Bora was fitted to those labels; the existing KODAMA–Bora + fastPLS and external predictions were remapped and rescored without refitting. MedSAM predicts only three prompted classes. These are development validation results on images inspected during earlier method development, and the methods do not share an identical prediction task.

Broad-label results on the same H&E image

The legend names only classes in the expert mask or at least 1% of a displayed model panel. Model overlays use the same Bora H&E tissue support. The KODAMA–Bora spatial mask is unavailable in the curated local cache, so that panel is deliberately unfilled; its score comes from exact frozen expert-by-prediction pixel counts.

The supervised Bora head selects 32 discriminative channels per class from the full 1,280-channel Virchow2 cache (203 unique channels), then fits a multiclass ridge classifier on the 397 training images. Four prespecified heads were compared on source-group-disjoint training folds using case macro IoU and CPU time; no development image was used for selection. KODAMA–Bora geometry and fastPLS naming were not refitted; their full confusion matrices were remapped to these broad labels. Their scores are therefore a reanalysis of the frozen output. The external predictions were similarly remapped and rescored without retraining, and MedSAM predicts only three prompted classes.

External method scores

U-Net and DeepLabV3+ use RGB H&E. VISTA-PATH uses external pretraining. MedSAM uses U-Net-derived boxes for tumor, stroma and necrosis. These methods have different output tasks; the cards do not establish a method ranking.
