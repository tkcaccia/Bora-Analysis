Supervised Virchow2 strategy comparison

Six ways to select and combine Virchow2 channels were compared on two source-group-disjoint held-out splits of the 397 training images. We chose one method before evaluating the 87 validation images. Frozen choice: 64 class-selected channels, eight PLS components, and a logistic head, with the prior adjustment selected on training data. On the 87 validation images its mean case expert-present macro IoU was 46.22%, versus 36.98% for the published 32-channel logistic mask. The paired gain was 9.24 percentage points (case-bootstrap 95% interval 6.24 to 12.53); 70 cases improved and 17 declined. Mean case Dice was 56.38%, recall 53.90%, and pixel accuracy 59.31%.

Nineteen PEsO validation images have only one expert-scored class, epithelial tissue, and account for much of the gain. Across the other 68 images, mean macro IoU changed from 38.22% to 41.02%, a 2.81-point gain (case-bootstrap interval 1.57 to 4.12). Per-dataset results and all cases are in the validation score table. The 87-image gallery was inspected during development and is not an untouched test. Missing feature tokens were filled from the nearest finite token in the same image; none overlapped harmonized expert-scored pixels. The panels above and the black-and-white maps below still show the original 32-channel model.

Training-selected Bora: start from 256 channels

A single set of 256 original Virchow2 channels was selected using only 397 training images and 1,192 expert-mapped training groups. This route starts with those channels before KODAMA, graph clustering, Bora's within-image 64-channel selection, mask fitting, and fastPLS tissue naming. Its fastPLS head was refitted on the same frozen training set using those channels. No evaluation image or whole slide supplied a feature-selection or classifier target. The historical training-group tile rule differs from this strict evaluation tile rule.

Black-and-white response maps for every tissue classification

For this image, each of the 24 black-and-white maps shows the response of a training-fitted classifier using only its 32 selected Virchow2 channels. Selection and fitting used the 397 training images and expert masks; validation expert masks and U-Net outputs were not used. Black is low and white is high on a shared 0–1 response scale. These values are not calibrated probabilities or accuracy scores. The feature grids were smoothed and interpolated to avoid visible token seams.
