Benchmark 1 · 67 additional paired H&E regions

Frozen Bora, eight-output U-Net and eight-output DeepLabV3+ were applied to 67 additional expert-annotated BCSS, TIGER and IGNITE regions. Results are separated into 30 regions from patients absent from the earlier development set and 37 further regions from development patients. No known training patient overlaps this cohort.

Retrospective extension. These images come from the same repositories used during method development. They are not a prospective, center-disjoint or untouched external test. Only positive expert tissue annotations enter the class and boundary scores; reference zero mixes unannotated tissue with background. The displayed method overlays use the same frozen H&E-only tissue support. The table below reports native predictions before clipping and common-support predictions separately.
