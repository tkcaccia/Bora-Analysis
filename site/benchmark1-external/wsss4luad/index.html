Interpretation and limits

Supervised Bora, U-Net and DeepLabV3+ use eight-class models fitted on Bora’s frozen 397-image training set. VISTA-PATH uses its fixed text vocabulary. MedSAM receives U-Net-derived boxes and returns overlapping binary prompt masks; its tumor and stroma values measure this guided combination, not an independent eight-class classifier. Gray in the overlays denotes a predicted class without a direct WSSS tumor/stroma mapping.

KODAMA–Bora produced anonymous pixel groups for 9 of 40 ROIs. The other 31 failed the frozen minimum of 40 tiles with strictly less than 10% background; their panels say “Not run.” Group colors have no tissue-name meaning. A fresh eight-class fastPLS group-naming refit failed its training-only rare-class viability gate, so no semantic KODAMA tumor score is shown.

The WSSS source reports 10× magnification. The displayed 1.0 µm/native-pixel spacing and the 5 µm boundary tolerance use that assumption; measured physical spacing was unavailable. U-Net, DeepLabV3+, MedSAM, VISTA-PATH and KODAMA outputs were projected from the registered doubled image grid to the native reference grid once by nearest neighbor. The common support is derived only from H&E, before reference scoring. Its background IoU is reported once because it is identical for all methods. The reference uses its own background code. These different model and prompt contracts do not support a single architecture ranking.
