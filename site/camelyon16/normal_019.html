Visual H&E review identified two tissue cores and pale adipose. The corrected common support contains 798,511 4-µm pixels. Of 652,254 official known pixels, 620,363 lie inside this tissue and form the score denominator; 31,891 outside are excluded. This support correction and reference inspection occurred before this current comparison, so this is a post hoc, reference-visible result.

Virchow2 encoded 1,168/329/98 tissue tiles at factors 1/2/4 with all 1,280 channels. The current U-Net and DeepLab processed 458 of 35,532 global patches that intersect this support. MedSAM received no boxes because the current one-slide U-Net predicted no class-2 pixels: its predeclared no-prompt output is zero metastasis and the MedSAM model was not invoked. VISTA uses its unchanged generic tumor prompt and reused same-slide, same-support audited output. These workflows have different prompt and pretraining dependencies.
