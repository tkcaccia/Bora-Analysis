False-positive burden on official known benign tissue

This slide has no reference metastasis. The scoring domain is 620,363 official known benign pixels inside the reviewed H&E tissue area (9.9258 mm²). 31,891 known pixels outside tissue are excluded; unannotated pixels are never counted as false positives. All values use a 4 µm × 4 µm grid. The percentage is false-positive pixels divided by known tissue pixels. Foci are eight-connected false-positive regions within this scored domain; only those at least 0.01 mm² are counted, while the largest-focus value includes any size. A focus cut by the annotation boundary can be smaller than the full prediction. The normal_019 tissue boundary was revised after visual review, so this is a post hoc tissue-only score. Native predictions are primary; shared-support outputs are a separate sensitivity analysis and can differ after contour processing. Metastasis IoU, Dice, precision, recall and lesion boundary F1 are undefined when the reference has no metastasis.
