Workflow and interpretation

The coarse mask is produced from expert annotations, then its class identities are anonymized. Virchow2 RGB conversion and Bora-AF refine these groups. The fastPLS classifier assigns tissue identities to whole refined groups using their Virchow2 feature means and contrast to neighbors weighted by physical shared-boundary length.

This controlled benchmark measures refinement and group identification given an expert-derived coarse map. It does not demonstrate annotation-free segmentation or independent clinical validation. All tissue is processed; unannotated or unmapped pixels are excluded from quality scores. Background is removed from final masks.

Polygon parts and enclosed holes with area strictly below 20 native pixels are removed or filled; exactly 20 is retained. The published Bora converter and calibrated shared-boundary fitting favor compact diagonal contours. Browser previews are resized for display; GeoJSON coordinates and the displayed µm/px calibration refer to the native ROI.

Cached deployment times include the recorded cleanup and boundary fitting, but exclude Virchow2 feature extraction, cold loading and model fitting. They are not end-to-end WSI runtimes.
