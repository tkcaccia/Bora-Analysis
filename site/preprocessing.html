Review the H&E tissue area

Trace the tissue boundary on the calibrated H&E image and export pixel-edge GeoJSON. Exclude exterior glass, large enclosed white voids and scanner regions; retain small enclosed adipose spaces. This is an image-only support mask, not an expert tissue-class reference. Review the overlay before admitting the image to a benchmark.

The GeoJSON must state the calibrated image dimensions, its SHA256, coordinate_space: calibrated_image_pixel_edges, and review_status: human_reviewed. The origin is the upper-left pixel edge, with X rightward and Y downward. An unrelated image hash, invalid polygon or out-of-frame geometry is rejected.

Make and verify the reusable mask

Use the benchmark command wrapper with an installed Bora Python package. The reusable rasterization and exact-pixel audit live in bora.r_tissue_support.materialize_reviewed_tissue_support. The wrapper creates a tiled TIFF and SUPPORT_VERIFIED.json in a fresh output folder.

python prepare_reviewed_tissue_support.py \
  calibrated-he.tif reviewed-tissue.geojson new-support-output \
  --minimum-free-gib 100

The receipt binds the image, reviewed vector, raster and pixel counts by SHA256. The program re-rasterizes the GeoJSON in bounded strips and checks every mask pixel and Virchow2 tile count. Pass the resulting TIFF or reviewed GeoJSON to Bora.preparation(tissue_masks = ...). For ordinary use without a mask, Bora encodes the full image including glass; benchmark images always receive precomputed support.

The retained CAMELYON16 pilot has mixed feature provenance: its two training slides reused full-field Virchow2 caches, including glass, with reviewed support applied downstream; its three test slides used support-admitted sparse extraction. A new masked extraction of the training pair would have different cache provenance and must be recorded separately; see the local pilot reproduction guide.

The Bora R package 0.1.4 has installation, API and one-result reproduction instructions. This page and its wrapper remain a local website draft; no new CAMELYON16 study or website publication follows from preparing a mask.
