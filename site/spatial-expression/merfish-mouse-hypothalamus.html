Native cell expression groups

The official Bioconductor source contains 73,655 segmented cells. The matching molecule resource overlaps 73,642 of them; all overlapping cells were analyzed. The 135 combinatorial smFISH genes supplied the KODAMA graph. Twenty-one analog features and five blank controls are retained locally but were excluded from the graph. Source cell-class annotations were retained for audit and were not used to fit groups.

Interpretation: These are exploratory expression groups. Their numbers do not identify cell types or pathology classes. The source values are fractional expression measurements and were preserved without integer rounding. Group 06 contains 395 cells and is mostly marked ambiguous in provider annotations; it needs cautious interpretation. No H&E image features, Bora polygon segmentation or class GeoJSON are claimed.
