Three native expression samples

The official Xenium human brain preview collection contains one non-diseased brain, one glioblastoma and one Alzheimer’s disease sample from three source donors. The disease names describe provider sample metadata; KODAMA did not predict diagnoses. All 110,248 native cells and 110,242 positive-gene cells are accounted for. Gene expression alone determined groups; native coordinates are used only for display.

Groups are exploratory and anonymous; group numbers are not cell types or pathology labels. The source uses a preliminary preview panel. No H&E Virchow2 features, Bora image refinement or class GeoJSON are claimed.

Verified analysis provenance · Official 10x dataset

The full native matrices, coordinates, reusable inputs and KODAMA fits are retained locally for all three samples.
