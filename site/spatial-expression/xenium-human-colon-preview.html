Four native expression samples

This official Xenium colon preview collection has non-diseased and cancer tissue, each with a base and an add-on gene panel. The four panel outputs represent two source donors; base and add-on panels are not independent biological samples. All 1,781,445 native cells and 1,780,931 positive-gene cells are accounted for. KODAMA used gene expression only. Source coordinates are used for display, not for clustering.

Groups are exploratory and anonymous. Their numbers are not cell-type or pathology labels. Low-count groups may reflect technical variation. No H&E Virchow2 features, Bora image refinement or class GeoJSON are claimed.
