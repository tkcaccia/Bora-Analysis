Full-slide overview: Bora and external methods

The eight-class ridge head was selected on source-group-disjoint training folds from 397 training images and uses 203 Virchow2 channels. For this slide, the training-selected 2% class-presence rule retains tumor, stroma, epithelium, blood, adipose, immune infiltrate; pixels from lower-area classes are reassigned by their next supervised Virchow2 score. The final mask is computed across the complete calibrated slide, smoothed at 6 µm, and repaired to remove class parts and enclosed holes below 2,000 µm². All ten views come from this one final mask and use the shared Bora tissue support for display. These are model predictions without a whole-slide expert mask.
