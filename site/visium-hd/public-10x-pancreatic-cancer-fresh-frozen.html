Full-slide overview: Bora and external methods

Eight tissue labels were frozen from 397 training images: tumor, stroma, necrosis, epithelium, blood, vessel, adipose, and immune infiltrate. Five sparsely represented leaf tissues have no scored label; their former predictions appear as untinted H&E. KODAMA–Bora geometry and four external-method predictions are unchanged and mapped to these broad labels. Supervised Bora uses a newly fitted eight-class Virchow2 head and 6 µm contour smoothing. All six overlays share Bora tissue support. No whole-slide expert masks are available, so these panels do not establish accuracy or method ranking.
